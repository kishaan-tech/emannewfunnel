Pre-Call Checklist

Do these before your call to get the most out of it.

  1. 1

    Take the call on a laptop, somewhere quiet

    We are going to talk through your goals, your product and your capital, and we do not run these calls with someone who is driving or between errands. Treat it like a meeting that matters, because the decision at the end of it does.

  2. 2

    Bring your questions

    Write down anything you want answered before you would commit to something like this. We would rather you make an informed decision than a fast one, and we will answer everything as straight as we can.

  3. 3

    Accept the calendar invite in your inbox

    This is what actually holds your slot. If it is not in your inbox, check promotions and spam.

    If the invite arrives as "Invitation from unknown sender", your mail client may show "I know the sender" instead of "Add to calendar". Click that first, then add it.
